THE PROBLEM AND THE SOLUTION

Why this application matters

Paper diaries make it easy to lose track of changing test times. A useful digital companion should make the schedule clearer and let patients discuss their recorded patterns with clinicians.

01

Know when to test

Follow a clinician-approved schedule: before breakfast daily, with other checks rotating between lunch, dinner and bedtime when prescribed.

02

Record readings directly

Enter the meter result manually in seconds. The reading is tagged with its date, time and meal context to support meaningful comparisons.

03

Understand the evidence

See averages, changes over time and missing measurements, without confusing descriptive statistics with diagnosis.

04

Stay in control

Optionally track supplements and explore cuisine preferences. Healthcare instructions and personal choices always take precedence.

  • Direct entry of fictional glucose readings

  • Numerical summaries and trend charts
  • 28 fictional scheduled test slots

Requires an external service or further validation

  • Real email and iPhone push delivery
  • Live large-language-model agent calls
  • Secure multi-user patient accounts and hosted database
  • Clinical safety, allergy and usability validation
  • Production monitoring and privacy impact assessment
